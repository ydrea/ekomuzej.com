about the ecomuseum

The ecomuseum is a model of heritage interpretation - natural as well as material intangible cultural heritage

    The basic characteristics of the ecomuseum are:

  • cover the area that extends beyond the walls;
  • fragmented location and on-site interpretation;
  • collaboration and partnership, rather than ownership of exhibitions or collections;
  • involvement of the local community in museum activities;
  • holistic, interdisciplinary interpretation.
